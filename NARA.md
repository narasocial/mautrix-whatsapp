# Nara's mautrix-whatsapp

This is the bridge [Nara](https://nara.contact) runs for testers who connect WhatsApp: [mautrix-whatsapp](https://github.com/mautrix/whatsapp) with a few patches, on the `nara` branch. It's published here under the AGPL-3.0, like the original.

The branch starts from upstream's v26.10 tag and adds:

- **Waiting for the full history sync** (`history_sync.wait_for_full_sync`). Portals aren't created after a login until the phone has sent the whole full history sync (a FULL payload with progress 100), or until that long after login. History that arrives after a chat's portal exists is otherwise lost on a homeserver without batch sending. Login bridge states say `info.history: "started"` (with `history_progress`) until every chat's portal and backfill are done, then `"done"`.
- **The `social.nara` block** on every message and edit event: whose WhatsApp it came through, the chat, the sender's LID and phone number, the receiver's own contact name for them, WhatsApp's message ID, and whether it came from history. See `pkg/connector/nara.go`.
- **A `redacted-stdout` log writer** (`cmd/mautrix-whatsapp/redactlog.go`): stdout with every phone number (any run of 7+ digits) replaced by a hash keyed per run, and names and message text dropped, for logs kept by a hosting provider.
- **[narasocial/mautrix-go](https://github.com/narasocial/mautrix-go)'s `nara` branch** in place of mautrix-go (`replace` in `go.mod`): a backfill skips a message the homeserver refuses (like one over the 64 KiB event limit) instead of ending the chat's backfill, and `Bridge.DeleteGhost` forgets a remote user.
- **Forgetting a human's chats** (`pkg/connector/naraforget.go`): `POST /_matrix/provision/v3/nara/forget` with `{"rooms": [...], "ghosts": [...]}`, which Nara's server calls after logging a login out. The bridge deletes any portal left for those rooms and what its state store cached of them, and each ghost it no longer has in use (not in a room it knows of, not a live login's own, not the sender of a message or reaction in a chat that stays), with its registration, its LID mapping and cached avatar unless a logged-in device knows them, and logged-out devices' privacy tokens. Nara purges the rooms and the ghosts' Matrix users on the homeserver. Every 10 minutes, and on every call, it also drops what the state store keeps of rooms with no portal that its bot has left. whatsmeow keeps LID mappings in memory too, until the bridge restarts.
- **Noticing the phone dropped the device** (`pkg/connector/naradevices.go`). WhatsApp can stop sending a linked device messages without telling it: the phone drops the device from its list, while the server goes on authenticating it and sending it receipts. Two minutes after each connection and then hourly, the bridge asks WhatsApp for the account's own devices (the usync query senders make) and looks for its own: in the server's device list, and with its key index valid in the phone's signed key index list (when that list isn't older than the link). When it's missing from either, it checks again 5 minutes later, and if it's still missing it reports `BAD_CREDENTIALS` with error `wa-device-unlisted`, which every later `CONNECTED` becomes too. It stays connected, so a logout still has WhatsApp remove the device and deletes its keys. `GET /_matrix/provision/v3/nara/devices/{loginID}` returns what the check sees (device IDs and key indexes, no numbers).

Nara builds its image from a pinned commit of this branch, the way upstream's Dockerfile does.
